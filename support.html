Purchases

Paid "100 Hearts" purchases are available in the iOS app via Apple In-App Purchase. If hearts didn't appear after payment, force-close and reopen the app — pending native purchases are recovered on launch when possible. If you were charged without receiving hearts, email us with your Apple order/receipt ID and we'll help.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

On the website, in-browser paid heart checkout is disabled (no server-side payment verification on this static site). Collect 100 hearts while playing, or buy them in the App Store build. If you were charged by Stripe for an older web checkout, email support@astryks.com with the email you paid with.

Common questions

My progress didn't save / I lost my best score. Progress and hearts are saved on your device only. Reinstalling the app or restoring your phone from a backup that predates your last session can reset local data.

The game crashed or froze. Please email us with your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing right before it happened.

I have a feature idea or found a bug. We'd love to hear it — email us any time.
