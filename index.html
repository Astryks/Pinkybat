FAQ

What is Becky the Bat?

A free casual arcade flyer. Fly Becky across famous cities, dodge obstacles, collect hearts, and chase distance.

Is Becky the Bat free?

Yes. Play free in the browser or get the free iOS app. Optional one-time 100 Hearts IAP can continue a run after a crash — never required to play.
